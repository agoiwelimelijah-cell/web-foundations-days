About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a minimalist web application designed to help you jot down ideas, reminders, and daily tasks without clutter or distraction. It runs entirely inside your web browser to provide an instantaneous and seamless writing experience whenever inspiration strikes.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type a brief title for your note in the "Note Title" field.
  2. Write your thoughts, checklist, or meeting notes inside the content area.
  3. Click the "Save Note" button to store your note instantly.

Features

  • Fast, distraction-free interface built with semantic HTML5 standards.
  • Instant browser-based note persistence with zero lag.
  • Full keyboard navigation and screen-reader accessibility.
  • Lightweight architecture that loads instantly on desktop and mobile devices.

Keyboard shortcuts
